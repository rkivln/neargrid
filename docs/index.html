Key Capabilities

Designed for Hyperlocal Community Trust

🌐

Geohash Spatial Grid

Automatically derives 7-character geohashes from device location to group neighbors into ~150m spatial cells without pinpointing house numbers.

📡

Broadcast Composer

Instant coordinate-pinned updates with quick category chips for Announcements, Local Meetups, Giveaways, and Safety Alerts.

⚡

Intent-Matching Engine

Connects local requests (Needs) directly with neighbor capabilities (Offers) in real-time within your active grid zone.

🛠️

Civic Issue Tracker

Report community issues like broken streetlights or hazardous potholes, upvote priority concerns, and track resolution progress.

📍

Safe Exchange Points

Discovers verified public exchange zones (e.g. Community Pavilions, Park Gates) for safe local trades and item handoffs.

🔑

Resident Pass Auth

One-tap passwordless identity powered by Jetpack Credential Manager and Firebase Google Sign-In.

Simple Workflow

How NearGrid Operates

01

Acquire Cell

NearGrid uses Android FusedLocationProvider to calculate your active 7-character geohash zone securely on-device.

02

Stream Feed

Subscribe to live Firestore listeners for your coordinate cell. Pull-to-refresh anytime for instant grid sync.

03

Engage Safely

Post local updates or meet up at designated public exchange points while preserving your home privacy.

Installation Steps

  • Download the NearGrid-release.apk file to your Android phone.
  • Tap the notification or open your browser Downloads folder.
  • If prompted, allow "Install from unknown sources" for your browser.
  • Open NearGrid and sign in with your Google account.
